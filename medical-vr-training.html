Quick answers

Can our medical, legal and regulatory teams review the content?

Yes. Storyboards and prototypes are shared for review before anything is built, so sign-off happens early, while changes are cheap.

Who checks clinical accuracy?

Our Senior Clinical Advisor works on medical projects alongside any subject-matter experts from your side.

Can we track trainee performance?

Yes. Experiences can record each step, score assessments and export results.

Which headsets do you build for?

Usually the Meta Quest lineup: standalone, affordable and easy to deploy. No PC or cables needed.

Can it run at congresses and events?

Yes, including a big-screen view so the crowd can watch while someone plays.
