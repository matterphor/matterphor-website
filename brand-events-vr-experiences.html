Quick answers

Will it match our brand?

Fully. Every experience is customisable to your brand’s style and preferences, down to the smallest detail.

Can it run at our events?

Yes. We build for busy stands, with quick sessions and a big-screen view so the crowd can watch.

Can we reuse it after the event?

Yes. The same experience can run at future events, in showrooms or for client meetings.

Which headsets do you build for?

Usually the Meta Quest lineup: standalone, affordable and easy to set up anywhere. No PC or cables needed.

How long does it take?

It depends on scope. We agree a timeline up front and you’re involved at every step, so it lands in time for your event.
