Quick answers

Can it follow our own procedures and HSE standards?

Yes. We build from your procedures, permits and standards, and you review storyboards before anything is built.

Can you build a digital twin of our site?

Yes. We work from CAD, drawings, scans or photos to recreate rigs, plants and equipment.

Can crews train together?

Yes. Multiplayer sessions let a whole team work through the same scenario, each in their own role.

Which headsets do you build for?

Usually the Meta Quest lineup: standalone, rugged enough for site and easy to deploy. Specialist hardware is available on bespoke projects.

Can we track trainee performance?

Yes. Experiences can record each step, score assessments and export results for your compliance records.
