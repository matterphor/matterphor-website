Quick answers

Does VR replace on-road training?

No, it complements it. Drivers arrive at on-road training already knowing the procedures and hazards, so time with an instructor goes further.

Can it match our vehicles and routes?

Yes. We build from your vehicles, depots and procedures so the experience matches your operation.

Can new starters use it during onboarding?

Yes. Standalone headsets can sit at every depot, ready for each new starter.

Which headsets do you build for?

Usually the Meta Quest lineup: standalone, affordable and easy to deploy. No PC or cables needed.

Can we track driver performance?

Yes. Experiences can record each decision, score assessments and export results.
