Quick answers

Can it run without an internet connection?

Yes. Experiences can be built to run on standalone headsets with no cloud connection required.

How many people can train together?

Multiplayer sessions are scoped to your team structure, from a pair of trainees to a full crew.

Can it match our exact equipment?

Yes. We build from your reference material, drawings or CAD so the virtual kit matches the real thing.

Which headsets do you build for?

Usually the Meta Quest lineup: standalone, affordable and easy to deploy. Specialist hardware is available on bespoke projects.

Can we track trainee performance?

Yes. Experiences can record each step, score assessments and export results for debriefs.
